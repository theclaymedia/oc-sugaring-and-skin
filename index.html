What is sugaring

An ancient ritual that is kinder to your skin

Dating way back to ancient Egypt, sugaring is the most gentle, natural and long lasting way to remove unwanted hair from any area of the body. It works for all skin types, hair textures and sensitivities.

  • 100% natural

    Our paste is only sugar, water and lemon. No resins, no chemicals, fully biodegradable. Sweet like candy, but we keep chocolate on hand for eating instead.

  • Truly sanitary

    Bacteria cannot grow in sugar. We wear gloves for every service and use a fresh ball of paste for every client, never double dipping.

  • Less painful

    Hair is removed in its natural direction of growth, so it slides out of the follicle instead of snapping. Sugar never grabs live skin cells.

  • Naturally exfoliating

    Every service lifts away dead skin cells too, so you leave with skin that feels soft, smooth and glowy.

  • Never hot

    Sugar paste is applied at body temperature. No burns, no scars and no bracing yourself before each pull.

  • Gentle and clean

    Water soluble, hypoallergenic and non comedogenic. Safe for faces and for clients with eczema, psoriasis or sensitive skin.

Client care

Get the most from every appointment

A little prep makes a big difference. Here is what we recommend before, during and after your visit.

Leading up to it

  • Let hair grow at least 1/4 inch
  • Exfoliate 2 days before, but not the day before or of
  • Skip sunbathing for 1 to 2 days

The day of

  • No lotion, oil or deodorant on the area
  • Arrive calm and on time. Less stress, less sting
  • Tylenol 20 minutes before can help

After and in between

  • For 24 hours, skip workouts, hot tubs and saunas
  • Exfoliate 2 to 3 days after, then 2 to 3 times a week
  • Moisturize daily and stay hydrated
Questions

Good to know before you book

Does sugaring hurt?

Most clients say it hurts much less than waxing. The paste only grabs hair, not skin, and hair is removed in the direction it grows. Many regulars find it gets easier with every visit.

How long does my hair need to be?

At least 1/4 inch, about the length of a grain of rice. Roughly 2 to 3 weeks of growth after shaving.

Can I come in on my period?

Yes. If you feel comfortable, please still come in. We provide wipes and tampons if you need them.

How often should I book?

Every 4 to 5 weeks is ideal for most areas. Staying on schedule keeps hair on the same growth cycle for smoother, longer lasting results.

Do you sugar clients under 18?

Yes. For bikini and Brazilian services, a parent or guardian must sign a waiver and be present at the first appointment. For clients 16 and under, a parent must be in the room.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours notice. Late cancellations are charged 50% of the service and no shows are charged in full. There is a 5 minute grace period for late arrivals. A card on file is required to book.
